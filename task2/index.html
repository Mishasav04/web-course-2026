The Club

Real coaching, no ego at the door. Ironclad exists to make boxing feel like it belongs to everyone who walks in — from a first jab to a first fight.

Training

Four rounds, one path — from your first jab to your first sparring session.

Round 01

Boxing School

Stance, footwork, first punches. No experience needed.

Round 02

Technique & Pads

One-on-one pad work, combinations, breathing.

Round 03

Conditioning

Bag intervals, rope, core — built for the long round.

Round 04

Sparring

Controlled pairs work, coach-supervised, by approval.
